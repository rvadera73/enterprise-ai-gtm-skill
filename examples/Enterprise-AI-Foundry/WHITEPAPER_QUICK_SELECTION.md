## The Specific Improvements V4 Makes

### 1. Publication-Quality Design
- Professional typography (Inter font hierarchy)
- Proper whitespace and margins
- Professional color scheme
- Like a printed business report, not a document

### 2. BLUF Section (Top of Page)
```

Organizations operating 5-8 independent AI systems hit an operational ceiling.
```

**Case 1: Healthcare**
- 5 specific systems, specific problems
- Extractable for LinkedIn post

**Case 2: Federal Agency**

- Shows federated ownership problem

**Case 3: Financial Services**
- Cost variation problem ($45K-$120K unexplained)
- CFO perspective (finance cares about this)

### 4. Professional Data Visualization
- Interactive chart (Project Mode vs. Platform Mode)
- Statistics boxes (15+ orgs, 50+ systems)
- Value cards (each layer gets visual treatment)

### 5. Institutional Voice
- Removed first-person ("I've seen")
- Changed to "Research across... reveals..."
- Sounds like research firm, not individual expert

### 6. Professional Layout
- Section dividers
- Callout boxes with consistent styling
- Layer indicators (visual labeling)
- Table of contents
- Proper footer with methodology note
