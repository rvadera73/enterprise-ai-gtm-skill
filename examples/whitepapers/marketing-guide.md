## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### Content Mistakes
❌ **Editing the post too much** — "The original sounds too salesy"
- Original posts are written to resonate. Trust the writing.

❌ **Skipping the engagement part** — "I'll post and let it sit"
- Engagement is where 80% of the value is. Comments = hot leads.

❌ **Waiting for perfection** — "Let me polish this first"
- Good enough + posted beats perfect + waiting. Iterate based on feedback.

### Execution Mistakes
❌ **Missing a day** — "I'll catch up with two posts tomorrow"
- Consistency matters more than catching up. One post per day, no exceptions.

❌ **Being too salesy too early** — "Let me mention our service"
- Week 1-2 is about building trust and establishing expertise. Sales comes later.

❌ **Not responding to comments** — "I'll get to them tomorrow"
- First 2 hours are critical for algorithm. Respond to comments same-day.

### Strategy Mistakes
❌ **Not tracking engagement** — "I'll just see what happens"
- Track: Who comments, what questions they ask, which posts perform best.

❌ **Treating all followers equally** — "Everyone on my feed is a prospect"
- Identify hot leads early. DM the CIOs and VPs who engage deeply.

❌ **Stopping after 2 weeks** — "LinkedIn didn't work"
- Week 1-2 builds foundation. Week 3-4 converts interest to meetings. Week 5-8 closes deals.

## Troubleshooting

**Q: The LinkedIn posts feel too long for LinkedIn. Should I shorten them?**
A: No. Long-form posts perform better on LinkedIn for thought leadership. You're competing on ideas, not brevity.

**Q: Should I post to multiple platforms (Twitter, Medium, etc.)?**
A: Start with LinkedIn only. Master one channel first. Once LinkedIn is working, expand (Week 3-4).

**Q: What if I don't get any comments on Day 1?**
A: That's normal. Your network is small on Day 1. Post anyway. By Day 7, you'll have more followers and better engagement.

**Q: Can I skip the engagement part and just post?**
A: Technically yes, but you'll get 1/10th the value. Engagement = algorithm boost + lead discovery. Don't skip it.

**Q: Should I soften the "problem exposition" posts?**
A: No. Stark problem statements are what get attention. Avoid being defensive; just be direct.

**Q: What if someone disagrees in the comments?**
A: Engage them respectfully. "Great point—here's how I think about that..." Disagreement drives engagement.

**Q: How do I know which commenters are real leads vs. casual followers?**
A: Real leads ask specific questions, mention their role, or challenge you thoughtfully. Casual followers give generic reactions. Track both but prioritize the specifics.

**Q: Can I reuse these materials next year?**
A: Posts and strategy stay relevant. Update dates and re-run the campaign with 20% tweaks.
