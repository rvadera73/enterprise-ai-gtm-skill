## Common Objections (Pre-Answered)

**"Won't this be too salesy?"**
- No. Posts focus on insights, not pitches. You're the expert. They'll ask.

**"Should I make changes to the posts?"**
- First time through: Use exactly as written. They're designed to work.
- After Week 1 feedback: Iterate for Week 2 based on what resonates.

**"What if no one engages?"**
- Check: Are you responding to comments? (This is the magic.)
- Check: Are your comments helpful, not generic?
- Check: Is your LinkedIn profile complete + professional?
- Check: Are you posting at 8-9 AM ET? (Timing matters.)

**"Should I post to Twitter/Medium too?"**
- No. Master LinkedIn first. Week 3+, expand if you want.

**"What if I miss a day?"**
- Post the next day. Don't try to catch up with multiple posts. Consistency > volume.

**"This seems like a lot of work."**
- 45 min/day for 2 weeks = 10.5 hours total. That's your entire GTM strategy. Worth it.
