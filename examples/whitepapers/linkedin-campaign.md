**Post Type:** Problem/Solution narrative

```

This is why enterprise AI systems stall:

A lawyer tells you: "The Ask-AI system has been giving incorrect answers about regulation X."

Your team investigates. The problem is:
→ The knowledge base was updated 3 weeks ago
→ The update was applied to LIVE production
→ You don't have a way to roll back
→ You don't know which queries were affected
→ You don't have a version history

This scenario repeats every quarter. Eventually, governance locks down updates ("no more changes without 6-week review process").

→ Updates to the knowledge base are tracked
→ Each update is tested before going live
→ You can instantly roll back if issues are found
→ You have full version history
→ Users can query against specific versions ("Show me this answer as of June 1")
→ Audit trails show exactly what changed

Cost of building versioning layer: $100K-$150K (one-time)
Benefit: No more "stale knowledge because we're afraid to update" problem

Most organizations treat versioning as a "nice to have."

Organizations at scale treat it as essential.

The difference:
- Without versioning: Update process is slow + risky (updates happen quarterly)
- With versioning: Update process is fast + safe (updates happen weekly)

Knowledge velocity is the real product advantage.
```

## Important Notes for Execution

### Tone & Voice
- **Specific > Generic** — Use real examples, not vague statements
- **Thoughtful > Salesy** — Help first, sell later
- **Systems thinking > Feature listing** — Talk about organizational patterns, not product features
- **Humble > Arrogant** — "Organizations are discovering..." not "We are the solution..."

### Posting Strategy
- **Post timing:** 8-9 AM ET (when CIOs check LinkedIn)
- **Comment timing:** Respond within 2 hours (algorithm boost)
- **Consistency:** Same general time each day (predictable to audience)

### Content Adaptation
- **Different angles each day** — Problem, evidence, solutions, organizational, strategic
- **Different engagement hooks** — Questions, challenges, data, stories, frameworks
- **Different audience segments** — CTO-specific, CISO-specific, VPE-specific posts

### Common Pitfalls to Avoid
❌ **Generic posts** — "Are you doing AI?" Get too specific.
❌ **Feature dumping** — Don't list features. Describe transformations.
❌ **Impatience** — Don't push for sales in week 1. Build relationships first.
❌ **Silence** — Every comment deserves a response. Engagement is where the magic happens.
❌ **Inconsistency** — Missing a day breaks the momentum.
