The Problem

Fragmentation

Each team builds its own Ask-AI system independently

Cost Explosion

15-20x the cost of a single system

Systems are built with project thinking (deliver a scope), not product thinking (continuous improvement).

When you scale from 1 system to 20, project thinking breaks.
