## How to Use

### Before Writing Any Content
1. Read: `/positioning/AUTHENTIC-MESSAGING-PROFILE.md` (your voice)
2. Read: `/positioning/POSITIONING-FRAMEWORK.md` (what to say)
3. Use: Your authentic voice + positioning framework

### LinkedIn Posts
- Write 2-3 high-quality thought leadership posts per month
- Match your voice (educational, contrarian, experience-based)
- Don't post generic templates
- Use: `/positioning/MENTAL-MODELS-POSITIONING.md` for mental model topics

### Blog Articles
- 2000+ words, deep exploration
- Educational focus (teach, don't sell)
- SEO-optimized keywords
- Use: `/strategy/LEAD-GENERATION-STRATEGY.md` for topics

---

## Content Guidelines

✅ **DO:**
- Match your authentic voice
- Lead with insight, not features
- Provide proof and examples
- End with questions, not CTAs
- Educate first, sell second

❌ **DON'T:**
- Use templates or generic language
- Start with "We've built..."
- List features without benefit
- Promote product, position framework
- Sound like a vendor
