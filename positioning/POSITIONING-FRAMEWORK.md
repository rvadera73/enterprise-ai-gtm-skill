## PART 1: ASK-AI SERVICE

### What It Is
A conversational intelligence system that integrates into existing enterprise applications without replacing them.

**Core Function**: Role-based AI agents that answer questions, analyze data, and surface insights from your existing systems.

### The Problem It Solves

Enterprise teams are drowning in knowledge fragmentation:
- Knowledge scattered across 5+ systems (SharePoint, Confluence, email, databases, case systems)
- Time cost: 2-4 hours/day per knowledge worker searching
- Operational cost: $50K+ annually per 1,000 employees in lost productivity
- Compliance cost: No audit trail on knowledge access

### How It Works

**Adapter Pattern**: Ask-AI has one core engine (conversation, analytics, knowledge base). Each host system gets a custom adapter that:
- Understands the system's APIs and workflows
- Defines role-based agent graphs (who sees what, what they can do)
- Routes questions to the right tools/data sources
- Returns answers with confidence + sources + audit trails

| **Production Deployment** | 28-day go-live (from RFQ to production) |
| **Accuracy** | 92% on enterprise queries (verified in pilot) |
| **Scale** | 600+ monthly active users across deployments |
| **Integration** | Works with existing systems (no replacement required) |
| **Use Cases** | Proven across: Case management (IACP), Trade enforcement (CBP), Knowledge management (Enterprise) |
| **Business Impact** | $150K+ annual help desk savings per 5,000-person enterprise |

### Value Proposition by Buyer

## PART 2: ENTERPRISE AI FOUNDRY

### What It Is
A unified framework for deploying multiple AI services across legacy systems with shared governance, compliance, and infrastructure.

**Core Function**: Reusable adapters + shared compliance layer + continuous intelligence deployment.

### The Problem It Solves

Enterprises face a modernization dilemma:
- Multiple legacy systems need AI capabilities
- Can't replace all systems at once (too risky, too expensive)
- Deploying AI services separately = fragmented governance, repeated compliance work, no reuse
- Traditional consulting: 18-24 months, $5-20M, high execution risk

### How It Works

**Three-Phase Pattern** (Proven across Federal and Commercial):

**Phase 1: Value Clarity (15 days)**
- Test if modernization is worth building
- Assess execution risk, timeline, cost
- Decision point: Proceed or pivot
- Cost: $50-100K

**Phase 2: Proof (30 days)**
- Deploy first AI service (Ask-AI, Risk Scoring, Document Management, etc.) to highest-priority system
- Measure: Accuracy, speed, business impact, user adoption
- Decision point: Expand or refine
- Cost: $100-300K

**Phase 3: Scale (60 days)**
- Add 2-3 more services to additional systems
- Reuse governance, compliance, infrastructure (cost per system drops)
- Full deployment across modernization roadmap
- Cost: $200-500K total

**Total Timeline**: 120 days (vs. 18-24 months)
**Total Cost**: $300-900K (vs. $5-20M)
**Risk**: Low (test before committing at each phase)
