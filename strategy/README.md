# GTM Strategy

Complete go-to-market strategies for Ask-AI and Enterprise AI Foundry.

## Files in This Folder

### 1. **LEAD-GENERATION-STRATEGY.md**
5-stage funnel for generating Sr. IT Executive leads:
1. **Awareness** — LinkedIn content about decision frameworks
2. **Consideration** — Lead magnet (decision framework)
3. **Deep Consideration** — Webinar/workshop
4. **Decision** — Direct outreach
5. **Conversion** — 30-day proof calls

**Use this**: To plan your complete lead generation motion.

### 2. **STRATEGIC-DISCOVERY-BRIEF.md**
Research synthesis on Ask-AI and Enterprise AI Foundry:
- What you actually have (product architecture)
- How they work together
- Competitor comparison
- Frameworks for positioning
- Open questions

**Use this**: As reference for discovery phase.

### 3. **DISCOVERY-INPUTS.md**
Input gathering template for positioning:
- Product information checklist

- Customer profile analysis
- Timeline and success metrics

**Use this**: When starting positioning for a new product or segment.

## Quick Reference

### Lead Gen Funnel Timeline
- **Week 1-3**: Content (awareness)
- **Week 3-4**: Lead magnet (consideration)
- **Week 4-5**: Webinar (deep consideration)
- **Week 5+**: Direct outreach & sales calls

### Lead Magnet
Decision framework: "Which modernization path is right for your enterprise?"
- Ask 5 questions
- Auto-generate recommendation
- Book 30-min call

### Success Metrics
- LinkedIn post reach: 10K+ impressions/month
- Lead magnet downloads: 50+/month
- Qualified calls booked: 5-10/month
- 30-day proofs started: 2-3/month
