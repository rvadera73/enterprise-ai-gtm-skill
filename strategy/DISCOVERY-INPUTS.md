## PRODUCT 1: ENTERPRISE AI FOUNDRY

### What It Is (User's Definition)
- **Marketplace + Managed Services Platform**
- For: Mid-to-large enterprises (500+ employee, both commercial and federal)
- Problem it solves: Technical debt (reducing 5+ year modernization to 90 days) + enables AI productivity
- Success metrics: Ideation to MVP in 15 days, Productionization in 90 days, 60-70% cost reduction, continuous compliance

### Current State
- **Product**: Production-ready
- **Available services**: 
  - Ask-AI (conversational knowledge)
  - Adjudication Case Management (production-ready)
  - Risk Scoring Model Management (MVP-ready)
  - 7-factor fraud ML model (production)
  - eCourt Document Management (MVP-ready)
- **Business models**: 2 proven models available
- **Sectors**: Federal focus but commercially viable for both

### Target Customer Profile

**Primary Buyer**: 
- Role: CTO, VP Engineering, Chief Architect
- Company: $500M+ revenue, 500+ employees
- Pain: Legacy application estate (5-10 year modernization roadmap)
- Budget: $2-5M annually for modernization/cloud migration

**Secondary Buyers**:
- Federal: GSA schedule holders, DoD/DHS prime contractors
- Commercial: Fortune 500 with digital transformation initiatives

**Buying Committee**:
- CTO (technology fit)
- CFO (cost reduction, ROI)
- CISO (compliance, security)
- Business Unit VP (time to market)

### The Real Problem (Beyond Generic "Technical Debt")

**Today's Reality** (Customer's Pain):
- Legacy ERP system runs critical finance processes (SAP, Oracle)
- Wants to modernize to cloud-native, AI-enabled architecture
- Current path: 5-7 year transformation, $50-100M cost
- Risk: Business changes faster than modernization happens
- Result: Stuck in a "treadmill" — never modernized, always patching

**What They've Tried**:
- Hired Accenture/Deloitte: $500K+ engagement fees, 18-month delivery
- Built internal teams: Now supporting legacy + building new (resource split)
- Cloud migration tools (Lift & Shift): Faster but still legacy architecture

**What They Actually Need**:
- Reduce modernization from 7 years to 90 days
- Reduce cost by 60-70%
- Maintain compliance throughout the transition
- Prove ROI on 1-2 services before committing to full platform

### Why Enterprise AI Foundry (Not "AI-DLC" or "AI Software Factory")

**The Offering**: Managed services marketplace + shared components
- **Managed Services**: Pre-built AI services (Ask-AI, Risk Scoring, Document Mgmt, Fraud Detection, etc.)
- **Shared Components**: Building blocks (RAG engines, model governance, compliance frameworks)
- **Marketplace**: Pick what you need, mix-and-match
- **Unified Platform**: Single compliance, governance, deployment model

**Differentiation vs. Alternatives**:
- vs. Accenture consulting: Weeks, not years
- vs. AWS/Azure AI services: Specific, not generic (built for enterprise outcomes)
- vs. Building yourself: De-risk (compliance already built in)
- vs. "AI Services à la carte": Governance nightmare without unified platform

### Positioning Context

**Market Category**: Enterprise AI Services Platform (not "Software Factory")

**Problem Hierarchy**:
1. **Tier 1 (Most Urgent)**: Reduce modernization timeline from years to months
2. **Tier 2**: Reduce cost by 60-70% compared to traditional consulting
3. **Tier 3**: Manage multiple AI services under one governance model
4. **Tier 4**: Achieve continuous compliance without reinventing (federal requirement)

**Proof Points**:
- 28-day Ask-AI deployment (production-ready)
- 7-factor fraud model built in 30 days (ready for production)
- 3 case management system available (adjudication + 2 others)
- Compliance architecture (federal sector validated)

- **Conversational knowledge platform** for enterprises
- For: Enterprise knowledge workers at 500+ person companies
- Problem: Find answers in 30 seconds (instead of 30 minutes across scattered knowledge)
- Stage: Production-ready
- Proof: 92% accuracy, 300+ active users, 28-day deployment

### Current State
- **Version**: v1.0 (production)
- **Deployment**: 28 days from RFQ to production
- **Accuracy**: 92% on customer queries
- **User base**: 300+ active users in pilot programs
- **Integration**: Works with SharePoint, Confluence, email, case systems (any knowledge source)

### Customer Profile (By Role, Same Enterprise)

**Primary: Knowledge Workers** (The End Users)
- Role: HR specialists, operations staff, finance analysts, customer service reps
- Pain: Spend 2-4 hours/day searching across 5+ systems for answers
- Benefit: Find answers in 30 seconds, be independent (less help desk)

**Secondary: HR/Ops Leader** (The Buyer)
- Role: VP HR, Director Operations, Finance Manager
- Pain: Help desk overwhelmed with "where do I find X" questions
- Benefit: Reduce help desk tickets 30%, new employees self-sufficient
- Metric: $50K+ annually in saved productivity

**Tertiary: CTO/CISO** (The Approver)
- Role: Chief Technology Officer, Chief Information Security Officer
- Pain: Knowledge in too many places, security risk, compliance liability
- Benefit: One interface, one governance model, audit trail
- Metric: Compliance + risk reduction

### The Actual Job to Be Done

**For Knowledge Worker**:
"Help me find an answer to my question without asking someone else"
- Job: Self-service knowledge access
- Current solution: Google + Slack + email + tickets (5+ systems)
- Ask-AI benefit: Conversational, cross-system, instantly

**For HR/Ops Leader**:
"Reduce the operational burden of knowledge management"
- Job: Enable self-service without losing control
- Current solution: Help desk team (expensive, slow)
- Ask-AI benefit: 70% of questions answered instantly, help desk focuses on complex

**For Enterprise (CTO/CISO)**:
"Centralize knowledge access with compliance and audit"
- Job: Single source of truth for organizational knowledge
- Current solution: Multiple systems, no audit trail
- Ask-AI benefit: Governed access, compliance-ready, one interface

### Positioning Context

**Market Category**: Enterprise Knowledge Platform (conversational AI)

**Problem Hierarchy**:
1. **Tier 1**: Reduce time to find answer from 2-4 hours/day to 30 seconds
2. **Tier 2**: Reduce help desk load by 30-50%
3. **Tier 3**: Consolidate knowledge access (5+ systems → 1 interface)
4. **Tier 4**: Audit knowledge access (compliance)

**Proof Points**:
- 92% accuracy on enterprise queries

- 300+ users, production-ready
- Works with existing systems (SharePoint, Confluence, email, etc.)
