# Marketing Production Skill Framework
**Building In-House Capability for High-Grade Marketing Assets**

---

## Overview: The Marketing Production Skill

**Goal**: Build internal capability to produce professional-grade marketing assets (images, videos, posts, whitepapers) without external agencies or designers.

**Scope**: 
- Strategic briefs and positioning
- Visual design (graphics, infographics, layouts)
- Video production (30-60 second clips, explainers)
- Social media content
- Publishing and measurement
- Iteration and continuous improvement

**Team**: Marketing lead + designer (can be trained) + video editor (can be trained) + analytics

---

## Part 1: Core Tools & Software Stack

### **Design Tools** (Pick One Primary)

**Option A: Figma (Recommended for scalability)**
- Cost: $12-$45/month
- Learning curve: Moderate (but trainable)
- Professional output: Excellent
- Collaboration: Built-in (multiple people can work on same design)
- Brand consistency: Built-in brand kit feature
- Why: Industry standard, templates available, cloud-based, no installation

**Option B: Canva Pro (Easier, faster)**
- Cost: $120/year
- Learning curve: Low (very intuitive)
- Professional output: Good (not premium)
- Collaboration: Limited
- Brand consistency: Brand kit feature available
- Why: Faster for social posts, built-in templates, easier learning curve

**Option C: Adobe Creative Suite (Professional, expensive)**
- Cost: $55-$85/month (full suite)
- Learning curve: Steep
- Professional output: Excellent
- Collaboration: Limited (Figma better)
- Why: Industry standard, but overkill for this work; too expensive for in-house

**Recommendation**: Start with **Figma** (1 seat) + **Canva Pro** (1-2 seats). Figma for brand-heavy, complex designs; Canva for quick social posts.

---

### **Video Production Tools**

**Option A: DaVinci Resolve (Recommended)**
- Cost: Free (or $295 one-time for Studio version)
- Learning curve: Moderate
- Professional output: Excellent
- AI features: Built-in color grading, noise removal, some AI-powered tools
- Why: Professional-grade editing, free, powerful, industry standard

**Option B: Adobe Premiere Pro (Industry standard)**
- Cost: $55/month (as part of Creative Cloud)
- Learning curve: Steep
- Professional output: Excellent
- Plugins/extensions: Extensive
- Why: Industry standard but expensive; overkill if DaVinci does the job

**Option C: CapCut (Easy, for short-form content)**
- Cost: Free (with watermark) or $50/year (no watermark)
- Learning curve: Very low
- Professional output: Good (for 15-60 second clips)
- AI features: AI cutout, filters, transitions
- Why: Very fast for short-form video; TikTok-optimized; great for learning

**Option D: Descript (AI-powered, emerging)**
- Cost: $24/month
- Learning curve: Very low
- Professional output: Good (AI-generated)
- AI features: Automatic transcript, speaker identification, AI voice, screen recording
- Why: Great for knowledge-worker videos (screen recording + voiceover); very fast

**Recommendation**: Start with **DaVinci Resolve Free** (learning) + **Descript** ($24/month) for quick voiceover/screen recording videos. Both can produce professional output quickly.

---

### **AI Image Generation Tools** (Emerging capability)

**For creating marketing images without hiring a designer:**

**Option A: Midjourney**
- Cost: $10-$120/month (depending on usage)
- Output quality: Excellent
- Use case: Conceptual graphics, backgrounds, illustration-style images
- Learning: Prompt engineering (learnable)
- Speed: 1-2 minutes per image

**Option B: DALL-E 3** (via ChatGPT Plus)
- Cost: $20/month
- Output quality: Very good
- Use case: Conceptual graphics, illustrations, design concepts
- Learning: Simple prompts work well
- Speed: 1-2 minutes per image

**Option C: Adobe Firefly** (via Creative Cloud)
- Cost: Included in Creative Cloud subscription
- Output quality: Good
- Use case: Backgrounds, fill objects, generative design
- Learning: Integrated into Photoshop/Illustrator
- Speed: Quick iteration in design tool

**Recommendation**: **Don't lead with AI image generation for this use case.** Why? Your brand positioning requires strategic visual communication (timelines, frameworks, proof points), not art. AI excels at creative/artistic imagery, not at communicating business concepts.

Instead: Use **Figma with templates** to design strategic graphics, then use **AI** to accelerate backgrounds, illustrations, or supporting elements.

## Part 2: Design Skill Development

### **In-House Designer Training Path**

**Assumption**: You have someone who can learn design, or you'll dedicate time to this.

### **Week 1-2: Figma Fundamentals**
- Learn Figma basics (shapes, text, components, frames)
- Create your brand kit (colors, typography, logos)
- Build 3-5 reusable templates (social post, image banner, infographic layout)
- Time commitment: 10-15 hours

**Resources:**
- Figma Design Fundamentals course (YouTube, free)
- Design+Code courses ($100-200, excellent)
- Figma community templates (learn by reverse-engineering)

### **Week 3-4: Strategic Design Briefs**
- Learn to interpret design briefs (you'll write these)
- Design 5 LinkedIn post images from briefs
- Create 2-3 whitepaper cover designs
- Get feedback, iterate
- Time commitment: 15-20 hours

### **Week 5-8: Specialized Designs**
- Infographics (timelines, frameworks, processes)
- Data visualizations (charts, metrics, proof points)
- Slide decks and presentation layouts
- Time commitment: 20-30 hours

**Total Learning Curve**: 60-80 hours to competency (2-3 months at 5-10 hours/week)

### **Brand Guidelines for Consistency**

Create a **brand design system** in Figma:

```
Brand Colors:
- Primary Navy: #013060
- Secondary Teal: #4AC4D3
- Accent Orange: #E6800C
- Light Blue: #DBF3F6
- Dark Teal: #0B6980
- Slate: #44546A

Typography:
- Headlines: Arial Bold, 24-36pt
- Body: Arial Regular, 11-14pt
- Captions: Arial 9pt

Spacing & Grid:
- 8pt base grid
- 16px padding/margin standard
- 12px gutter between columns

Imagery Style:
- Professional, not playful
- Solution architecture aesthetic
- No stock photos of people smiling
- Diagrams over decorative illustrations
- Data-driven visuals

Logo Usage:
- Min size: 40px
- Always with clear space (10px around)
- Never distort or change colors
```

### **Template Library (Start Here)**

Create 5-10 reusable Figma templates:

1. **Social Media Post** (1200×628px LinkedIn)
2. **LinkedIn Carousel** (1200×1500px, 5-10 slides)
3. **Blog Header Image** (1200×400px)
4. **Infographic Frame** (1200×1600px vertical)
5. **Timeline Visual** (1400×800px horizontal)
6. **Framework Diagram** (1200×1000px)
7. **Quote Graphic** (1080×1080px Instagram)
8. **Whitepaper Cover** (1280×1656px)
9. **Video Title Card** (1920×1080px, 16:9)
10. **Email Banner** (600×200px)

**Each template includes**:
- Brand colors locked in
- Typography locked in (but text editable)
- Grid/spacing guides
- Component variants for consistency

## Part 3: Video Production Skill Development

### **In-House Video Editor Training Path**

**Assumption**: You have someone to learn editing, or you'll dedicate time.

### **Week 1-2: Editing Fundamentals (DaVinci Resolve)**
- Learn timeline-based editing (cut, trim, arrange clips)
- Color grading basics
- Audio mixing basics
- Export settings
- Time commitment: 8-12 hours

**Resources:**
- DaVinci Resolve official tutorials (YouTube, free, excellent)
- CaseyFiesler editing course ($50-100)

### **Week 3-4: Video Script & Storyboard Interpretation**
- Learn to build a video from a written script and storyboard
- Understand visual pacing and timing
- B-roll management
- Voiceover sync
- Time commitment: 12-16 hours

### **Week 5-8: Specialized Video Types**
- Explainer videos (30-60 second)
- Testimonial videos (product/customer)
- Screen recording + voiceover
- Animated graphics (using DaVinci or external tool)
- Time commitment: 20-30 hours

**Total Learning Curve**: 50-70 hours to competency (2 months at 5-10 hours/week)

### **Video Production Workflow**

**Step 1: Script & Storyboard** (You provide)
- Written script with timing
- Shot list (what to film/use)
- Visual direction (tone, pacing)

**Step 2: Gather Assets**
- Screen recordings (if software demo)
- B-roll (if available)
- Audio files (if voiceover provided)
- Graphics/animations (from Figma or AI tool)

**Step 3: Assemble in Editor**
- Import to DaVinci Resolve timeline
- Arrange according to script
- Sync audio and video
- Add transitions and effects

**Step 4: Color Grade & Polish**
- Color correction (match all clips)
- Audio mixing (levels, EQ)
- Add titles/graphics
- Export at correct resolution

**Step 5: Quality Check & Iterate**
- Watch full video
- Check timing against script
- Verify audio sync
- Check brand consistency

### **Video Templates (Pre-Built Projects)**

Create 3-4 DaVinci Resolve templates:

1. **Explainer Template**
   - Title card (3 sec)
   - Problem statement (10 sec)
   - Solution intro (10 sec)
   - Proof/demo (20 sec)
   - Call-to-action (7 sec)

2. **LinkedIn Post Template**
   - Hook text (3 sec)
   - Main content (25 sec)
   - CTA (2 sec)

3. **Screen Recording Template**
   - Intro title (2 sec)
   - Screen recording with voiceover (40-50 sec)
   - CTA (3-5 sec)

4. **Testimonial Template**
   - Intro (2 sec)
   - Quote/claim (15-20 sec)
   - Proof (20 sec)
   - CTA (3 sec)

## Part 5: Production Schedule & Workflow

### **Weekly Marketing Production Cadence**

**Monday: Strategy & Briefs**
- Meet with marketing lead (30 min)
- Agree on messaging, proof points, audience
- Write design brief for image
- Write video script brief
- Assign to designer and video editor

**Tuesday-Wednesday: Production**
- Designer creates image from brief (4-6 hours)
- Video editor records voiceover, gathers assets (4-6 hours)
- Both iterate based on feedback

**Thursday: Review & Refinement**
- Review video first edit (30 min meeting)
- Review design options (30 min meeting)
- Request revisions or approve
- Export final assets

**Friday: Publish & Measure**
- Publish LinkedIn post with image
- Publish video (LinkedIn, YouTube)
- Set up analytics tracking
- Document performance

### **Output Per Week**
- 1 LinkedIn post with custom image
- 1 short-form video (30-60 sec)
- 1 blog post or long-form content (optional)
- 2-3 quote graphics for sharing
- Analytics tracking on all assets

### **Monthly Goals**
- 4 LinkedIn posts
- 4 videos
- 1 whitepaper or long-form content
- 8-12 supporting graphics
- Clear measurement of engagement

## Part 6: Measurement & Iteration

### **Metrics to Track**

**Per LinkedIn Post:**
- Views
- Engagement rate (likes + comments + reposts)
- Click-through rate (to website/whitepaper)
- DM inquiries
- Comment sentiment (positive, neutral, critical)

**Per Video:**
- Views
- Watch time (average %)
- Engagement rate
- Shares
- Click-through rate

**Overall GTM Campaign:**
- Traffic to website (from LinkedIn)
- Whitepaper downloads
- Demo requests / DM inquiries
- Content performance trend (which topics resonate)

### **Iteration Loop**

**Weekly Review** (30 min):
- Which posts had highest engagement?
- What messaging resonated?
- What should we repeat, what should we change?

**Monthly Review** (1 hour):
- What content drove actual conversions (demos, customers)?
- Which topics are resonating?
- Adjust messaging/positioning for next month
- Update brand guidelines if needed

**Quarterly Review** (2 hours):
- Overall GTM effectiveness
- Content that converted vs. didn't
- Competitive positioning shifts
- Update strategy and messaging hierarchy

## Part 7: Getting Started (First 30 Days)

### **Week 1: Setup & Learning**
- Purchase tools: Figma ($45/month), Descript ($24/month), DaVinci Resolve (free)
- Identify designer and video editor (internal hire or contractor)
- Create brand style guide in Figma
- Build 3 design templates
- **Output**: 0 public-facing assets (setup phase)

### **Week 2-3: First Content Piece**
- Write design brief for LinkedIn post (based on published Ask-AI post)
- Designer creates 3 options
- Select best, finalize image
- Record voiceover script
- Video editor assembles 30-second clip
- **Output**: 1 LinkedIn post + 1 video

### **Week 4: Second Content Piece & Documentation**
- Repeat process for second LinkedIn post topic
- Document workflow (what worked, what didn't)
- Train team on process
- Build analytics dashboard
- **Output**: 1 LinkedIn post + 1 video + documented workflow

### **Week 5+: Rhythm & Scale**
- Publish 1 LinkedIn post per week
- Publish 1 video per week
- Build content calendar for next 8 weeks
- Measure and iterate

## Conclusion: Building Marketing as a Core Skill

This framework transforms marketing from **outsourced vendor work** to **internal core capability**. 

- **Tools are affordable** ($50-200/month)
- **Skills are learnable** (60-80 hours to competency)
- **Workflow is repeatable** (once established, scales easily)
- **Measurement is clear** (which content works, which doesn't)
- **Iteration is continuous** (improve each week)

The goal is not to hire ad agencies or outsource to freelancers. The goal is to build a 2-3 person marketing production team that can:
- Interpret strategy
- Produce high-grade assets
- Measure effectiveness
- Iterate continuously
- Scale to multiple content pieces per week

**By Q1 2027, you should be publishing 4-8 high-quality marketing assets per week across LinkedIn, video, whitepapers, and case studies—all produced in-house with no external dependency.**
