### 6. **Sales Conversation Framework**

**Feature**: Scripts and structure for closing 30-day proofs

**What It Includes**:
- **Opening** (30 seconds):
  - Validate their concern
  - Challenge their assumption
  - Show alternative

- **Problem Exploration** (3 minutes):
  - Questions that reveal execution risk
  - Questions about timeline, cost, disruption risk
  - Listen for implicit concerns

- **Your Solution** (5 minutes):
  - The pattern: 15-day clarity → 30-day proof → 60-day scale
  - Proof: Ask-AI at IACP, CBP, Enterprise
  - Why it works: De-risks with each phase

- **Objection Handling**:
  - "It will take 6 months" → "Ask-AI integrated in 15 days"
  - "Our data is unique" → "That's why we customize in days"
  - "We need compliance" → "Governance is built in"

- **Closing** (1 minute):
  - Clear ask: "Let's prove this in 30 days on your priority system"
  - Low risk: "Pilot before you commit"
  - Next step: "Can we schedule the kickoff?"

**Files**:
- `/positioning/MENTAL-MODELS-POSITIONING.md` (sales psychology)
- `/documentation/SALES-CONVERSATION-GUIDE.md` (when created)

**Why This Matters**: Removes guessing from sales calls. Everyone on your team uses the same proven script.
