## Part A: Design Brief (LinkedIn Post Image)

### **A1. Strategic Intent**

**Post Context**: "From 90-Day MVPs to 15-Day Value Proposition"  
**Audience**: Federal program managers, commercial CTOs, startup founders  
**Goal**: Stop scroll, communicate that Enterprise AI Foundry enables rapid MVP validation  
**Tone**: Professional architecture, data-driven, not marketing fluff

### **A2. Visual Concept: "Timeline Compression"**

**What it communicates**: 
- Traditional enterprise delivery takes 18 months
- Enterprise AI Foundry compresses to 90 days (with 15-day MVP)
- Compliance is embedded throughout, not bolted on at the end

**Key Visual Elements**:
1. **Left side (Traditional)**: 18-month timeline with compliance at the end (shows bottleneck)
2. **Right side (Enterprise AI Foundry)**: 90-day timeline with compliance embedded
3. **Cost comparison**: $3M-$3.5M vs. $300K-$500K
4. **System examples**: Four systems (Ask-AI, Risk Scoring, Case Management, eCourt) with brief icons
5. **Headline**: "From 90-Day MVPs to 15-Day Value Proposition"

### **A3. Layout & Composition**

**Dimensions**: 1200×628px (LinkedIn landscape optimal)

**Visual Hierarchy**:
1. Headline (top, largest): "From 90-Day MVPs to 15-Day Value Proposition"
2. Timeline comparison (center, primary visual)
3. Cost metrics (bottom left)
4. System examples (bottom right)
5. Brand logo (corner)

**Layout Grid**:

### **A4. Brand Guidelines (Fixed)**

**Color Palette**:
- Primary Navy: #013060 (backgrounds, text)
- Secondary Teal: #4AC4D3 (highlights, arrows, emphasis)
- Accent Orange: #E6800C (call-outs, metrics)
- Light Blue: #DBF3F6 (backgrounds)
- Dark Teal: #0B6980 (borders, secondary text)

**Typography**:
- Headlines: Arial Bold, 28-32pt
- Subheaders: Arial Bold, 18-22pt
- Body text: Arial Regular, 12-14pt

**Style**:
- Clean, grid-based layout
- No photos, only diagrams/graphics
- Data-driven visuals (charts, timelines, frameworks)
- Professional, architectural aesthetic

### **A6. Production Workflow: Design**

**Option 1: AI-Generated Image (Fastest)**
1. Use prompt above with Midjourney or DALL-E
2. Generate 3-4 variations
3. Select best, refine prompt if needed
4. Export as PNG 1200×628px

**Option 2: Custom Design in Figma (Most Control)**
1. Create project in Figma
2. Use brand kit (navy, teal, orange)
3. Build timeline comparison side-by-side
4. Add cost metrics and system examples
5. Export as PNG 1200×628px + keep Figma file for edits

**Option 3: Hybrid (Recommended)**
1. Generate base timeline comparison with AI (Midjourney)
2. Import into Figma
3. Add text, logos, system examples in Figma
4. Polish and finalize

**Timeline**: 2-4 hours (AI generation is 30 min, editing is 1-3 hours)

### **A7. Reference Examples**

**Look Like** (Professional, clean, data-driven):
- Gartner Magic Quadrant visuals (clean grid, clear categories)
- McKinsey/BCG strategic frameworks (organized, hierarchical)
- Stripe/GitHub architectural diagrams (minimal, purposeful)

**Don't Look Like** (Avoid):
- Stock photo marketing collages
- Cluttered with logos and decorative elements
- Cheesy gradient backgrounds
- Overly illustrative or cartoonish

### **B3. Video Production Specifications**

**Format & Dimensions**:
- Resolution: 1920×1080 (16:9 landscape)
- Frame rate: 30fps
- Codec: H.264
- File size: <50MB
- Platform: LinkedIn (also works for YouTube, Twitter)

**Audio**:
- Voiceover: 16-bit, 44.1kHz stereo
- Background music: Subtle, professional (no cheesy marketing music)
- Music options:
  * Epidemic Sound (subscription, professional)
  * Artlist (subscription, professional)
  * YouTube Audio Library (free, limited)
  * Unsplash Music (free, creative commons)

**Suggested Music**: Calm, modern, instrumental (think: tech company presentation music)
- Genre: Ambient, lo-fi, or minimal electronic
- BPM: 80-100 (measured, not rushed)
- Duration: 35-40 seconds to fill 30-second video with slight fade

**Captions**:
- **CRITICAL**: LinkedIn auto-plays without sound, add burned-in captions
- Font: Arial Bold, white (#FFFFFF) with navy (#013060) background bars
- Timing: Synchronized with voiceover
- Position: Bottom 30% of frame (safe for mobile viewing)

### **B4. AI Video Production Tools**

**Option 1: Descript (Recommended for Speed)**
- Cost: $24/month
- What it does: 
  * Record or upload voiceover
  * Auto-generate captions
  * AI edit from script (auto-cuts silent moments)
  * Export final video
- Timeline: 2-3 hours
- Quality: Good (simple motion graphics)

**Option 2: Synthesia**
- Cost: $30/month
- What it does:
  * AI generates video from script
  * Choose AI presenter (or no presenter)
  * Auto-add captions
  * Custom backgrounds and branding
- Timeline: 1-2 hours
- Quality: Good (AI video can feel AI-generated, but works for explainers)

**Option 3: DaVinci Resolve (Pro, Most Control)**
- Cost: Free or $295 one-time
- What it does:
  * Professional video editing
  * Color grading, effects, animations
  * Full control over every frame
- Timeline: 4-6 hours
- Quality: Excellent (but requires skill)

**Recommendation for 30-Second Video**: Use **Descript**
1. Write voiceover script
2. Record or use AI voiceover (Descript has built-in)
3. Import storyboard visuals (screenshots, AI-generated graphics)
4. Let Descript auto-edit to voiceover timing
5. Add background music
6. Export MP4
