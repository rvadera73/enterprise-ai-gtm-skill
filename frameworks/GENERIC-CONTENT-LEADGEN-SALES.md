## Phase 4: Generic Lead Generation

### By Product Stage (Not Product Type)

Lead gen motion changes based on STAGE, not what you're selling.

**Pre-Launch Stage**:
```
Goal: Build awareness + waitlist

Channels:
├─ Founder LinkedIn (personal credibility)
├─ Content marketing (problem education)
├─ Community engagement (find early adopters)
└─ Cold outreach (early customers)

Messaging:
- "We're solving [problem]. Want to be an early user?"
- Focus on problem, not solution (problem validation phase)

Metrics:
- Waitlist growth: 100+ per month
- Customer interviews: 10+ per month
- Early adopter conversations: 5+ per month
```

**Growth Stage**:
```
Goal: Qualified leads → pilots → customers

Channels:
├─ Thought leadership (LinkedIn, blog)
├─ Lead magnet (decision framework, calculator)
├─ Webinar (education + lead gen)
├─ Direct outreach (sales development)
└─ Inbound (SEO, content)

Messaging:
- "Here's the pattern we've proven..."
- Focus on proof, not claims

Metrics:
- Monthly leads: 50+
- Qualified leads: 10+
- Pilot proposals: 3-5
- Conversion rate: 20-30%
```

**Mature Stage**:
```
Goal: Account-based marketing, expansion

Channels:
├─ Thought leadership (industry positioning)
├─ Strategic partnerships
├─ Analyst relations
├─ Account-based marketing (targeted)
└─ Executive outreach

Messaging:
- "Here's how the category is evolving..."
- Focus on category leadership

Metrics:
- Enterprise deals: $500K+
- Expansion revenue: 30%+ of new
- Analyst coverage
```

### Generic Funnel Structure (5 Stages)

```
Stage 1: AWARENESS
├─ Goal: Visibility with target buyer
├─ Tactics: LinkedIn content, blog, industry presence
├─ Duration: Weeks 1-3
└─ Metric: 10K+ impressions, 50+ comments

Stage 2: CONSIDERATION
├─ Goal: Deep engagement with qualified leads
├─ Tactics: Lead magnet (decision framework), webinar
├─ Duration: Weeks 3-4
└─ Metric: 50+ lead magnet downloads, 30+ webinar attendees

Stage 3: EVALUATION
├─ Goal: Direct conversations with decision makers
├─ Tactics: 1:1 outreach, consultation calls, proof approach
├─ Duration: Weeks 5-6
└─ Metric: 10+ qualified calls, 3+ pilot proposals

Stage 4: DECISION
├─ Goal: Close pilot or proof
├─ Tactics: Pilot agreement, SOW, success metrics
├─ Duration: Weeks 7-8
└─ Metric: 2-3 pilots signed

Stage 5: EXPANSION
├─ Goal: Expand within customer, add more use cases
├─ Tactics: Implementation success, case studies, expansion sells
├─ Duration: Ongoing
└─ Metric: 20-30% of revenue from expansion
```

## Phase 5: Generic Sales Framework

### By Buyer Psychology (Not Product)

Sales conversation changes based on WHO DECIDES, not what you're selling.

**Technical Buyer (CTO, VP Engineering)**:
```
Opening (30 sec): Validate execution risk
"Most modernization projects take 18+ months and disrupt production. 
I help technical leaders prove they can do it safely in 90 days."

Exploration (3 min): Uncover execution risk
- "How many legacy systems are you managing?"
- "If modernization goes wrong, what happens?"
- "What would make you confident you could do this in 90 days?"

Solution (5 min): Address execution risk
"We've proven this approach 3 times: 15-day clarity, 30-day proof, 60-day scale.
Zero system replacement. Low execution risk."

Objection Handling:
- "It will take 6 months" → "Ask-AI integrated in 15 days. CBP in 3 weeks."
- "Our data is complex" → "That's why we customize in days."
- "We need compliance" → "Governance built in."

Closing: Clear ask
"Let's prove it on your highest-priority system. 30 days. If it works, you scale."
```

**Financial Buyer (CFO, VP Finance)**:
```
Opening (30 sec): Validate cost concern
"Most enterprise modernization costs $5-20M and takes years. 
I help CFOs cut that by 70% without cutting corners."

Exploration (3 min): Uncover financial pain
- "What's budgeted for modernization this year?"
- "If you could cut that cost by 60%, what could you do with the savings?"
- "What's holding you back from starting today?"

Solution (5 min): Address cost concern
"$300-900K for what consulting charges $5-20M.
Faster. Cheaper. Same outcome."

Objection Handling:
- "Hidden costs?" → "Fixed timeline, fixed cost. $100K for proof."
- "Too good to be true?" → "Reusable framework, not custom engineering."

Closing: Clear ask
"30-day proof at $100K shows the math works. Scale from there."
```

**Business Buyer (Business VP, Operations Leader)**:
```
Opening (30 sec): Validate business outcome
"Your team spends 2-4 hours/day searching for answers. 
I help you eliminate that and free up your people for higher-value work."

Exploration (3 min): Uncover operational pain
- "How much time does your team spend on knowledge search?"
- "What could they accomplish if they had that time back?"
- "What's holding you back from solving this?"

Solution (5 min): Address business outcome
"Reduce search time from hours to seconds.
Help desk cost drops 30%. Employee satisfaction goes up."

Objection Handling:
- "Will our people use it?" → "92% adoption in pilot. Works like Google."
- "How long to implement?" → "28 days. No disruption."

Closing: Clear ask
"30-day pilot shows the business case. $150K+ savings clear in month 1."
```

## How to Apply to a New Product

When you have a new product (e.g., "Fraud Detection ML Platform"):

1. **Framework Selection** (5 min)
   - Product Type: Enterprise AI Service
   - Stage: [Pre-launch / Growth / Mature]
   - Target Buyer: [Financial institution risk officers]
   - → Look up framework in matrix

2. **Run Positioning Process** (6 weeks)
   - Follow same 9 exercises
   - Apply selected framework
   - Validate with customer interviews
   - Produce 23 strategic artifacts

3. **Customize Content** (2 weeks)
   - Use content calendar template
   - Customize for fraud/risk topic
   - Customize messaging for Chief Risk Officer buyer

4. **Build Lead Gen** (1 week)
   - Use funnel template for Growth stage
   - Customize messaging to target buyer
   - Build webinar, lead magnet

5. **Create Sales Framework** (1 week)
   - Build conversation for Chief Risk Officer buyer type
   - Handle objections relevant to risk/fraud space
   - Closing ask specific to product

**Total**: 10 weeks from new product to full GTM system

## Key Insight: Generic Process, Infinite Applications

The **process is identical** for all products:
- Same 9 discovery exercises
- Same 23 artifacts to produce
- Same funnel structure
- Same sales conversation framework

The **customization** happens at:
- Framework selection (which framework to use)
- Buyer type messaging (what to emphasize)
- Topic customization (fraud vs. knowledge vs. case management)
- Timeline/stage customization (pre-launch vs. growth)

This is why one framework can serve 100 products.
