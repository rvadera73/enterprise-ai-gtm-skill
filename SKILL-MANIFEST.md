## Skill Overview

A comprehensive, framework-driven go-to-market skill that works for ANY enterprise product (SaaS, DevTools, Platforms, AI Services, Government products). Uses proven positioning methodologies (JTBD, Geoffrey Moore, April Dunford, Value Prop Canvas, Category Design) to create positioning, content strategy, lead generation, and sales frameworks automatically.

### What It Does

Takes a product description and automatically:
1. **Selects the right framework** (based on product type × stage)
2. **Runs a 6-week positioning workshop** (9 discovery exercises)
3. **Generates 23 strategic artifacts** (positioning, personas, messaging, brand guidelines, sales playbooks)
4. **Creates content strategy** (customized by buyer type)
5. **Builds lead generation plan** (customized by product stage)
6. **Develops sales framework** (customized by buyer psychology)

### Why It's Different

- **Generic**: Works for Ask-AI, Risk Scoring, eCourt, OR any enterprise product
- **Framework-Based**: Uses proven positioning methodologies, not templates
- **Reusable**: Same process for all products; customization is explicit
- **Scalable**: Can be applied by your team or licensed to other organizations
- **Practical**: Outputs actionable artifacts (not theoretical documents)

## Skill Features

### 1. Framework Selection Engine
**Automatically recommends the right positioning framework based on**:
- Product type (DevTools, SaaS, Platform, Enterprise AI, Government)
- Product stage (Pre-launch, Growth, Mature)
- Target buyer profile
- Market conditions

**Ensures**:
- Correct framework for your situation
- Proven methodology (not guessing)
- Time-appropriate approach (pre-launch vs. growth have different needs)

### 2. Generic 6-Week Positioning Workshop
**Works identically for all product types**:
- 9 discovery exercises (Future State Headlines, Problem Definition, Personas, etc.)
- Framework application (customized by type)
- Validation process (customer interviews, competitive analysis)
- Segmentation & messaging
- Operationalization (producing 23 strategic artifacts)

**Customization points**:
- Which exercises get more weight (pre-launch emphasizes problem validation; growth emphasizes differentiation)
- Which framework applied (selected by framework engine)
- Which messaging emphasis (by buyer type)

### 3. Content Strategy Generation
**Creates thought leadership content customized by buyer type**:
- CTO/Technical Buyer: Architecture, integration, technical case studies
- CFO/Financial Buyer: ROI, cost comparison, financial outcomes
- Business Leader: Operational efficiency, team productivity, business impact
- Individual User: Ease of use, time savings, job fit

**Outputs**:
- 3-month content calendar
- Post structure (LinkedIn, blog, email, video)
- 10+ content topic ideas
- Lead magnet concept

### 4. Lead Generation Funnel
**Creates 5-stage funnel customized by product stage**:
- **Pre-Launch**: Founder credibility + waitlist building
- **Growth**: Demand generation + product-led growth
- **Mature**: Account-based marketing + partnership expansion

**For each stage**:
- Messaging approach
- Tactics (content, webinars, direct outreach)
- Expected metrics
- Lead qualification criteria

### 5. Sales Framework & Scripts
**Creates conversation frameworks customized by buyer psychology**:
- **Technical Buyer** (CTO, VP Engineering): Focus on execution risk, architecture, timeline
- **Financial Buyer** (CFO, VP Finance): Focus on cost, ROI, implementation cost
- **Business Buyer** (VP Operations, Business Leader): Focus on business outcome, team productivity
- **Executive Buyer** (C-suite): Focus on strategic fit, competitive advantage, risk

**For each buyer type**:
- Opening (30 seconds)
- Problem exploration (3 minutes)
- Solution narrative (5 minutes)
- Objection handling
- Closing strategy

### 6. 23 Strategic Artifacts Model
**Produces complete, actionable deliverables**:

| Category | Count | Examples |
|----------|-------|----------|
| Positioning | 5 | Statement, Differentiation, Value Prop, Brand Promise, Brand Guidelines |
| Personas | 6 | 3 personas + 3 persona-specific value props |
| Messaging | 7 | Tier 1, 2, 3 messages + 3 segment-specific messages + hierarchy + do's/don'ts |
| Strategy | 5 | Competitive positioning, USP, Category definition, Market positioning, Sales strategy |
| **Total** | **23** | Complete GTM foundation |

### 7. Application Examples
**Included examples**:
- Ask-AI (Enterprise AI Service, Growth stage)
- Risk Scoring (Enterprise AI Service, MVP stage)
- eCourt (Government SaaS, Pre-launch)

**For each example**:
- Framework selection rationale

- Key differences from other examples

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Can I use this for a B2B SaaS product?
**A**: Yes. The framework selection engine will recommend Geoffrey Moore (growth stage) or Category Design (mature). Same skill, different framework.

### Q: What if my product is in a new category?
**A**: The skill includes guidance for emerging categories. Use Category Design framework to define your category first.

### Q: Can I use this for multiple products?
**A**: Yes. That's the core strength. Run the skill for Ask-AI, then for Risk Scoring, then for eCourt. Same process, different outputs.

### Q: How long does it really take?
**A**: Framework selection = 5 min. Full positioning = 6 weeks (with guidance). Content + lead gen = 2-3 weeks once positioning is done.

### Q: Can I license this to other organizations?
**A**: Yes. The skill is generic and reusable. Could be offered as a service or licensed methodology.

### Q: What if I don't know my target buyer?
**A**: The skill includes an exercise to identify buyer personas. Run that first, then use skill for positioning.
